FAQ

Before you volunteer as evidence.

Is this online dog training?

No. The product is entertainment: a personalized roast based on your own dog video. Any useful observation that sneaks into the roast is not how the product is marketed.

Will my video be posted publicly?

Not unless you separately opt in. Permission to process your submission and permission to feature it publicly are separate choices.

Do I need an account?

Not for the first version. The planned flow is video + email + consent, with the result delivered through a secure link. Accounts can be introduced later for history, subscription management and repeat uploads.

Is the roast made by AI?

At launch, the finished roast is human-produced. Automation is introduced gradually as the reaction library and video-understanding system become good enough to preserve the voice instead of flattening it.

Can I submit someone else’s dog?

Only upload material you have the right to submit. The app will require confirmation that you are allowed to provide the video for processing.
