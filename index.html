FAQ

Before you volunteer as evidence.

Is this online dog training?

No. You are buying entertainment: a personalized roast based on your own video. If something in the roast makes you suddenly reconsider what you’re doing, that is between you and your conscience.

Will my video be posted publicly?

Not unless you separately opt in. Permission to process your submission and permission to feature it publicly are treated as separate choices.

Do I need an account?

Not for the first MVP flow. The plan is video + email + consent, with the finished roast delivered by a secure link. Accounts can come later for history, subscriptions and repeat uploads.

Is the roast made by AI?

At launch, the finished roast is human-produced. Automation will be introduced gradually as the reaction library and video-understanding system become good enough to preserve the style instead of flattening it.

Can I submit someone else’s dog?

Only upload material you have the right to submit. The app will require confirmation that you are allowed to provide the video for processing.
