Your video is not public by default.

Last updated: 21 August 2026.

OnlyInsults is being prepared for launch. This page describes the intended MVP data flow and will be updated with the final legal entity/controller details before public submissions are enabled.

What we collect

For the first DOGS submission flow we expect to collect the video you upload, your email address, optional context you provide, consent records, technical/security logs and the finished roast associated with the submission.

Why we use it

We use submission data to receive and process your request, create and deliver your roast, protect the service from abuse, provide support and meet legal obligations.

Public use is separate

Submitting a video for a roast does not automatically give us permission to publish it on TikTok, Instagram, the website or other public channels. Public featuring requires a separate opt-in.

AI and product development

If submission data is ever used to improve or train automated systems beyond what is necessary to provide the requested service, the applicable notice and consent basis will be made clear in the product. Public-use permission and model-improvement permission are not assumed from a basic submission.

Storage and deletion

Videos and generated results will be stored only for the period needed to operate the service, provide the result, handle support and meet applicable legal/security requirements. Retention periods will be finalized before public launch.

Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights including access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection. Contact privacy@onlyinsults.net.
